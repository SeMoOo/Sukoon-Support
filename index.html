Quick troubleshooting

  • Prayer times: Verify your selected city, time zone, calculation method and manual corrections. Follow your trusted local timetable if schedules differ.
  • Qibla: Verify location permission or select a city manually. Keep the device away from magnetic interference.
  • Notifications and Athan: Check iOS notification permission, reminder and sound preferences and Focus settings.
  • Personal data: Use Sukoon's Privacy & Data screen and review the privacy policy.
